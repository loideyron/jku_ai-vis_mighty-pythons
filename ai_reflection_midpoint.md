# AI-Aided Pair: Reflection on Phase I
by: K12147981: Vladyslav Protsko
    K12111267: Simon Stromberger

This reflection summarizes our experience using AI assistance for the [dataset and model-behavior overview](phase1/AI-aided/dataset-context-model-behaviour.ipynb) and the focused [investigation of multiple-action requirements](phase1/AI-aided/multiple_actions_investigation.ipynb).

AI assistance supported our analytical choices, visualization development, and revisions. For example, in the multiple-actions investigation, it helped develop the comparison between requirements annotated with multiple actions alone and those with additional reasons. It also helped propose clarification edits paired with paraphrase controls and build linked exploration tools. These contributions broadened the investigation beyond the initial overview.

Our role increasingly involved evaluating the AI’s suggestions rather than implementing every detail ourselves. We questioned whether interactive features added analytical value, selected which comparisons to retain, and asked for simpler explanations. Having AI generate visualizations did not remove the need for us to understand and justify the analysis. AI made more complex interactive visualizations easier to implement. For simpler plots that we could have implemented ourselves, it also felt substantially faster to generate an initial version. However, the generated code and outputs still needed review and refinement.

The assistance also introduced additional work. We had to question unnecessary interactivity and review the suitability of proposed analyses. In the multiple-actions investigation, this included reviewing proposed edits and labels, as well as correcting rendering problems and duplicated content after repeated execution. Some explanations also needed shortening. The assistant’s initial recommendations were not always the simplest suitable solution.

For future work, we would give AI focused tasks, check its suggestions against the data, and keep interactive features only when they help the analysis.
