# AI Reflection (Midpoint)

## Further investigation of Multiple-Action Requirements

This reflection summarizes my experience using AI assistance for the [multiple-actions investigation](../phase1/AI-aided/multiple_actions_investigation.ipynb). 

AI assistance helped me develop the comparison between requirements annotated with multiple actions alone and those with additional reasons. It also helped propose clarification edits paired with paraphrase controls, build linked exploration tools, and implement revisions. These contributions broadened the investigation beyond the existing overview.

My role increasingly involved evaluating the AI’s suggestions rather than implementing every detail myself. I questioned whether interactive features added analytical value, selected which comparisons to retain, and asked for simpler explanations. Having AI generate visualizations did not remove the need for me to understand and justify its analysis. AI assistance helped me implement more complex interactive visualizations than I could have built on my own. For simpler plots that I could have implemented myself, it also felt substantially faster to generate an initial version. However, the generated code and outputs still needed review and refinement.

The assistance also introduced additional work. I had to question unnecessary interactivity and review whether the proposed edits and labels were appropriate. Rendering problems and duplicated content after repeated execution required corrections, and some explanations needed shortening. The assistant’s initial recommendations were not always the simplest suitable solution.

For future work, I would give AI focused tasks, check its suggestions against the data, and keep interactive features only when they help the analysis.
